This repository presents a curated portfolio of Power BI projects focused on solving real business problems through analytics, data modeling, and automation.

The projects reflect scenarios commonly found in corporate environments, including large data volumes, multi-source architectures, automated refresh cycles, and report distribution workflows.

---

## Portfolio Scope

The portfolio focuses on:
- Sales Operations and pipeline quality management
- Forecasting and performance tracking
- Revenue analysis and commission-related reporting
- Executive and management-level decision support
- Automation using Power BI and Power Automate

All projects were designed with global scalability, governance, and operational adoption in mind.

---

## Projects

### Forecast & Pipeline Quality

forecast-pipeline-quality/forecastpreview.gif

This project focuses on improving sales pipeline hygiene, forecast reliability, and accountability.

It combines pipeline quality KPIs with automated, individualized insights delivered to Account Managers using Power Automate, enabling better qualification, follow-up, and forecast accuracy.

### Sales Performance

sales-performance/performancepreview.gif

A global Sales Performance Dashboard designed to track attainment, revenue evolution, targets, and commission-impacting metrics.

The project includes a dedicated Manager View with ranking and comparative performance indicators, enabling leadership to quickly identify top performers and performance distribution across teams.

## Architecture and Automation

Both projects are built on enterprise-grade BI foundations:
- Centralized Data Warehouse
- Direct CRM integration
- Internal operational datasets
- Star schema data models
- Automated refresh cycles
- Power Automate–based report distribution

Automation plays a key role in connecting insights to action through scheduled and event-driven report delivery.

---

## Data Volume and PBIX Availability

Due to large data volumes, multi-source architectures, and corporate governance constraints, PBIX files are not shared in this repository.

Instead, each project is demonstrated using:
- Animated GIFs showing dashboard interaction
- Visual walkthroughs of Power Automate workflows
- Examples of automated email delivery to end users

This reflects realistic corporate BI scenarios without compromising data privacy or performance.

---

## Data Privacy

- All data shown is public, simulated, or fully anonymized
- Customer and Account Manager names are replaced with generic identifiers
- No real company, client, or confidential information is disclosed
- Projects are not connected to production environments

## Purpose of This Portfolio

This portfolio was created to demonstrate:
- Practical Power BI expertise in corporate-scale scenarios
- Strong understanding of sales, performance, and operational processes
- The role of analytics in improving data quality and decision-making
- End-to-end BI solutions that combine data, insight, and automation
