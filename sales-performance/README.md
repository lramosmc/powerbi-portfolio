
# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project delivers a **global Sales Performance Dashboard** designed to serve as a recurring monthly reference for Account Managers, Sales Leaders, and Management.

The dashboard provides a consolidated and transparent view of individual and overall sales performance, with a strong focus on targets, revenue evolution, and metrics that directly influence commission and performance evaluation.

Similar to Project 1, this solution was built for **company-wide adoption at a global level**, ensuring standardized KPIs, consistent definitions, and reliable performance comparisons across regions.

---

## Business Purpose
The primary goals of this project are to:
- Track sales performance against targets
- Monitor progress toward annual goals
- Enable fair and transparent commission calculations
- Support recurring performance reviews
- Provide management with fast, reliable visibility into team results

The dashboard acts as a trusted performance source, reducing manual reporting and alignment discussions across the sales organization.

---

## Key Metrics and KPIs

### Core Performance Metrics
- Attainment YTD
- Sales YTD – Current Year
- Average Sales – Current Year
- Sales Year-over-Year (YoY)
- Sales YTD – Previous Year
- Average Sales – Previous Year

These metrics allow users to assess both absolute performance and relative evolution over time.

---

## Target vs Sales Analysis
A monthly comparison of targets versus actual sales results provides visibility into:
- Over- and under-performance trends
- Seasonality effects
- Consistency of goal achievement throughout the year

This view supports proactive performance management rather than retrospective analysis only.

---

## Customer and Growth Analysis

### Sales by Customer
- Revenue breakdown by customer
- Visibility into customer concentration
- Support for portfolio balance analysis

### New Logo Sales
- Revenue generated from new customers
- Visibility into growth driven by customer acquisition
